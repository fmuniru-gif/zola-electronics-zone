GOOD TO KNOW

Before you buy.

Helpful answers, without the fine-print surprises.

Can you help me choose between different products?+

Yes. Tell us what you're shopping for, your budget and how you intend to use it. We can discuss suitable options and important differences.

Do your products come with a warranty?+

Warranty coverage and terms vary by product and brand. Please ask us to explain the specific terms and available support before buying.

Can I trade in my old television?+

You're welcome to enquire. Share your TV's brand, size, age, condition and photos if available. We can advise whether a trade-in assessment is currently possible. Acceptance is not guaranteed.

Can I discuss the price with you?+

Of course. Tell us your budget and what you need. We'll discuss suitable options and any current offers; final prices are confirmed by the shop.
